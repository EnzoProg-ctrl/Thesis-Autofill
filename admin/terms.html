Safety Terms

A report that contains an active danger term is raised to at least High priority, whatever the AI predicted. It can only raise a priority, never lower it. Exclusion phrases are ignored before matching, so “test our smoke detector” is not treated as smoke.

Changes apply to new reports only. Tenant pages pick them up when they next load; the AI server within about 5 minutes. Existing reports keep the priority they were given.
